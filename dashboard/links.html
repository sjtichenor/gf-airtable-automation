Client reports

How fresh the numbers are

One snapshot is held in memory and rebuilt every . Straight after a deploy the first build takes about a minute and a half and the page says “warming up” until it lands. Views are lifetime totals per post as last synced, not views within the range.

If a client link 404s

A client page exists only once its slug appears in CLIENT_PASSWORDS on gf-api. The selector variable alone is not enough — both have to carry the same slug, which is why a new report is a two-variable job.

Adding a client

Three ways to define one: a show (the slug is the show name), a named set of accounts via CLIENT_GROUPS, or a word in the clip title via CLIENT_MATCHES. Editor, director, poster and client-account fields are removed before anything leaves the server, whichever you use.
